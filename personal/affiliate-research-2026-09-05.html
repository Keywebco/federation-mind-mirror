Top 5 Picks for the Federation (Ranked by ROI Potential)

1. Copy.ai - 45% Recurring for 12 Months

Why it's #1: The highest recurring percentage among AI tools with a 90-day cookie window. Federation visitors are already AI-curious creators. A single $49/mo referral = ~$264 over 12 months. Place this on any AI tools page, the Sovereign Library, or Roger AI Sim recommendations. Zero conflict with Federation values since Copy.ai serves independent creators.

2. ConvertKit (Kit) - 50% for 12 Months

Why it's #2: Creator-first email platform with the highest commission rate in the email marketing category. Federation philosophy = build your own audience, own your list. ConvertKit is literally the tool for that. Every Federation page about sovereignty or independence should link to ConvertKit as "the tool to own your audience." 90-day cookie means casual visitors who come back weeks later still count.

3. Payhip - 50% Recurring Lifetime

Why it's #3: The Federation sells digital products. Payhip is the anti-corporate alternative to Gumroad with LIFETIME recurring commissions at 50%. Any page that mentions "sell your own digital products" should link to Payhip. This is pure philosophical alignment: sovereignty, independence, no-subscription-trap.

4. Notion - $50 + 20% Year-One Revenue

Why it's #4: The 180-day cookie is the longest in this entire list. Every serious creator/builder uses Notion. The hybrid payout ($50 bounty + 20% of their first year) can easily exceed $100 per referral. Place on any productivity/tools resource page. Universal appeal means high conversion.

5. AppSumo - 100% Commission Up to $50

Why it's #5: One hundred percent commission on new customers is unheard of. AppSumo's audience = indie creators and SaaS enthusiasts, which is the exact Federation demographic. The 7-day cookie is the weakness, but the conversion rate on deal-hungry visitors is high. Best placed in "recommended tools" roundup posts or newsletters.
