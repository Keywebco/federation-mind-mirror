Catalyst's Attack — Five Holes Found

Hole 1: Drive is NOT in the forever layer

Pontus placed Drive in the forever layer. This is incorrect. Drive lives on Google's servers under keywebco@gmail.com — the same account controlling YouTube and any new GCP OAuth clients. One account suspension, billing lapse, or policy flag takes Drive, YouTube, and OAuth credentials down simultaneously. Drive belongs in the borrow layer alongside YouTube. GitHub-only is the true forever layer.

Hole 2: Composio is an invisible single point of failure

Neither Roger nor Pontus named it. The entire automation pipeline (GitHub pushes, Drive reads, Gmail, scheduling) runs through Composio. If Composio goes down or billing lapses, orchestration stops completely. Content survives in GitHub but nothing generates or publishes new content. Required mitigation: a documented manual-push procedure Roger can execute from any browser without Composio.

Hole 3: The verifier cannot be the publisher

The same pipeline cannot publish AND verify its own output — this is structurally identical to the ghost-count failure. The verification step must be a separate, independent scheduled check: different agent, different trigger, no shared state with the publish flow. YAML write-back happens only after the independent verifier confirms. Never before.

Hole 4: Spotify owns the listener relationship

The content is portable. The audience is not. MP3 files can be exported. Subscriber relationships cannot — listeners follow the Spotify show, not a portable RSS URL. If Spotify goes hostile, the audience relationship is lost even though the content survives. Partial mitigation: every episode description must include a direct RSS link pointing to the GitHub-hosted feed, training listeners to the sovereign source over time.

Hole 5: WordPress.com free injects Automattic's ads

On the free plan, Automattic injects their own advertising into Roger's content pages. Additionally, the WordPress.com free newsletter subscriber list is governed by Automattic's terms — they retain rights to contact subscribers. This is a sovereignty breach. Use WordPress.com as an SEO mirror only. Never as the subscriber relationship home.
