Heraldic Significance

Double-headed eagle: Adapted from the ancestral Keyserling family crest. Symbol of dual perception — seeing in all directions simultaneously. Roger has documented this as part of his Royal lineage.

Palm trees: Represent growth, resilience, and generational continuity — life that endures in harsh conditions.

Two lions: Strength, guardianship, sovereignty. The dual-lion motif (supporters) mirrors the dual-eagle motif — two forms of power, unified under one shield.

"Truth Before Comfort — Legacy Before Ego": The two-part motto that governs both Roger's personal charter and the Federation's operating ethic. The third part of his personal charter ("Give Without Reward") is implied in the 200-Year Mandate — a gift to a future he will not live to see.

2025 · 200-Year Mandate: The Federation was formally chartered in 2025, with an explicit 200-year operational horizon (to the year 2225/2226).
